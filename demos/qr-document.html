QR transport document

Spain's Sustainable Mobility Law 9/2025 asks for a digital transport document that a roadside inspector can scan. This demo walks through the flow I built: start a route, get the document and its QR code, then scan it.

Demo only. This is a small simulation written for this site. It runs in your browser. All names, plates and numbers are made up. It uses no Bringg code and no Bringg data.

1. Route details

Tip: press Start route twice with the same Route ID. The second press shows how a retry never creates a second document.
